FAQ

Good to know

Anything else? Message us on WhatsApp and we'll help.

What are your hours?

We're open every day, Monday to Sunday, from 8am to 5pm.

Is there an age limit?

There's no legal age limit, because the rides take place on private property. Kids and families are welcome.

Where do we meet?

At Wushwini Art Centre in Ngcolosi, near Inanda Dam, Durban, KwaZulu-Natal.

How do I pay?

Online when you book. Checkout runs through Umzila, Durban's marketplace for the best local businesses, with secure card payments through PayFast. You get an email confirmation straight away.

Can I book on WhatsApp instead?

Yes. Send your ride length, day and start time to Umzila's booking line on WhatsApp and we'll sort it out.

Can I use a gift voucher?

Yes. Vouchers work storewide at Nceks — any ride length or merch — and any balance left stays on the code. Open the link in your voucher email, or enter the code at checkout.

What should I wear?

Closed shoes and clothes you don't mind getting dusty.
