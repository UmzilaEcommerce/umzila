For Durban's local businesses

Your own store page. Our riders deliver.

Kitchens, shisanyamas, bakers, makers and experience businesses sell on Umzila. Customers order and pay online, and an Umzila rider collects from you and delivers.

  1. ApplyTakes about 2 minutes.
  2. We WhatsApp youWithin 24 hours, to get to know your business.
  3. Set up your storeOnce approved, you get an email to create your store, your link (umzila.store/yourstore), products and opening hours.
  4. Get ordersYou're told the moment an order is paid. Get it ready; a rider picks it up.
